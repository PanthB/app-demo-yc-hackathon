# Glass

Owner: APPD-12. The cards and list views, the detail sheet and the standard item every view shares, drawn with system text styles, system backgrounds and Apple's Liquid Glass. They read the same `Drop` records as the board view in `ios/Board`.

- `CardsView(sections:namespace:select:)` is the default view: sections from `DropSection`, pinned drops as a row of compact cells, every other section as a two-column masonry grid, and done collapsed at the end. It owns its scroll view, with a hard top edge so cards don't show through the bar. At accessibility text sizes the grid is one column and pinned drops are ordinary cards. With no sections it shows `EmptyBoard`. A long press opens a context menu that calls `dropActions` (pin, mark done, put it back, move to, open link). Every item carries `matchedTransitionSource` (clipped to the card's shape) and `matchedGeometryEffect` with the drop's id in the namespace it's given.
- `ListView(sections:namespace:select:)` is the compact view: a native inset grouped `List` with the same sections and headers, done collapsed until its header is tapped, a hard top edge and `EmptyBoard` when there are no sections. Rows swipe (pin or unpin on the leading edge, mark done on the trailing edge), long press for the same context menu, and carry the same `matchedTransitionSource` and `matchedGeometryEffect`.
- `DropRow` is the list's row: a 56pt thumbnail (a clip's poster gets a play badge), the title in up to two lines, one meta line ("reading this one now." while a drop arrives, else when and where, else the site), and the kind pill and the saver's avatar on the trailing side.
- `DropDetailView(drop:)` is the detail sheet's content: the big preview with its pill, the title, the meta line, the suggestion, who saved it and when ("maya saved this 2 hours ago."), the site, and a bottom bar of Liquid Glass buttons in one `GlassEffectContainer`: pin, move to and mark done as glass circles, and the main action as `.glassProminent` (listen, watch, visit or open when there is a link, else mark done). Marking done closes the sheet.
- `DropMenu(drop:)` is the one context menu (pin, mark done, put it back, move to, open link) used by cards and rows. `ZoneMenu(drop:)` is its list of sections to move to, which the detail's move button also uses.
- `DropCard` is the standard card: the preview, the kind pill at the top leading corner, the title, one meta line (when, then where), the suggestion when there is one, and who saved it. A drop with a picture shows it full width. A drop without one is drawn on the card's own surface: a note's words, a reminder's live countdown, an event's date, a voice note's waveform and length. A clip's poster carries a play badge with its length, and so does a song's artwork once the length is known.
- `PinnedCard` is the compact cell in the pinned row, every cell the same height with the title in up to two lines and a pin mark beside it, and `DropThumbnail` is the small square it and the list row lead with: the picture, or the same face drawn small (the date block, the live digits, a short waveform, a note's first words). A drop with only a title leads with the title.
- `KindPill`, `PinBadge` and `ClipBadge` are the only glass on a card (`glassEffect` in a capsule). Glass needs a picture behind it, so `onPicture: false` draws the pill as a plain system fill on a card's own surface. `KindPill(symbol:word:)` draws the same pill for a kind that isn't a drop's, such as a suggestion to watch. `PersonAvatar` is the saver's initial on their `authorColor`. `SuggestionLine` is the one line the app says about a drop, in the accent color: the backend's words, "reading this one now.", or a live "starts in 40 minutes." ("that's in 40 minutes." for a place).
- `DropKind` works the kind out from fields `Drop` already has: a due time makes a reminder, anything that plays (a video, a TikTok, YouTube, Vimeo or Instagram reel link, or an Instagram post with a length) is a clip, then what the reader found (song, event, place), then the source (photo, voice, link, note).
- The meta line is when, then where: a song adds its artist and genre, a clip its maker and site, a place its name and neighborhood. The genre is `genre`, or the genre word in `vibes`, and the neighborhood is the one neighborhood name in `vibes`. A drop with no title and no picture yet says what it is and whose ("a link from sam").
- `Masonry` is the pure column maths: each card goes to the shorter column, and a section of up to 12 cards is split so the two columns end as level as they can while the first card stays top leading.
- `GlassBoardRoot` was the stand-in host for the cards and list views until the shell (`BoardShell` in `ios/App`) landed. Nothing shows it now, so it can be deleted once nothing in flight still names it. It shows the drops of `BoardsStore.currentBoardId` under that board's name (in DEBUG, `-BoardDebugBoard <id>` shows another board, and a seeded launch shows the seed's own board even on a fresh install, before the store has caught up with the seeded person), grouped by vibe on a shared board, as `ListView` when the view mode is list and `CardsView` otherwise, and presents `DropDetailView` in a full height sheet with the zoom transition from the tapped item. Its tint follows the appearance, #C9472B in light and #FF6B4A in dark, so accent text passes 4.5:1 on both.
- `SuggestionsFeed(boardId:)` is a screen of its own: what the backend found for a board, live from `suggestions:list`, grouped by cluster with the largest first. A `SuggestionCard` shows a thumbnail, the kind pill, the title, one meta line (who it's by, when, where, the price), one line about it, then why it's here: the backend's reason and the one or two drops that led to it, drawn from the device's own copy of the drop when it has one. Three buttons: "save to board" (`suggestions:save`, then a short "saved to sf crew." note), "dismiss" (`suggestions:dismiss`) and "open" (the page it came from). A cluster that's being searched says so in one quiet line. The toolbar menu holds the two recs settings, kept in `@AppStorage` (`recsMedia`, `recsEvents`), sent through `people:setPrefs` and read back from it when the feed opens; with both off the feed says so and offers to turn them back on. It needs a `NavigationStack` around it (for the title and the menu) and `BoardsStore` in the environment: push it, or present `NavigationStack { SuggestionsFeed(boardId: id) }` in a sheet.
- In DEBUG, `-BoardDebugSuggestionsSample full` swaps the network for a built-in sample (five suggestions in sf crew's three clusters) and `-BoardDebugSuggestionsSample empty` for an empty feed. On a seeded launch (`-BoardDebugSeed YES`) the feed still reads live, but save, dismiss and the settings stay on the device, so a test run never writes as a seed person.

Rules this folder follows: a card is opaque content with a 22pt continuous corner, no tilt and no glow. Text is SF Pro in system text styles, so Dynamic Type works, and numbers use monospaced digits. The accent color (`.tint`) marks what is live: countdowns, the date on an event, a waveform and the suggestion. A person's color appears only on their avatar.

Compiled into the app target by folder (see `ios/project.yml`), so a new file here needs no project edit. Markdown files are excluded from the build. `ios/UITests/CardsViewUITests.swift` and `ios/UITests/ListViewUITests.swift` drive these views through the `AppDemoUITests` target. They pick drops by kind and position, not by name, so a reseed doesn't break them. `ios/UITests/SuggestionsFeedUITests.swift` drives the feed on its sample, through the shell's `suggestionsButton`, and skips itself while that button shows something else.
